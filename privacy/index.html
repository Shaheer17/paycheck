Privacy Policy

Paycheck collects nothing. Your numbers stay on your phone.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Paycheck is a budgeting app. You type in your own numbers, and the app works out what you can afford. This policy explains what happens to those numbers.

The short version

Your numbers stay on your phone. We don't collect them, we can't see them, and we don't have a server that stores them.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • Your name and birthday, if you choose to add them. Both are optional. Bill uses them to greet you and to wish you a happy birthday
  • Your balance, payday and expected pay
  • Your bills, savings goals, wishlist and spending budget
  • The spending you log, including names and categories
  • Your settings, such as your currency and Bill's hat

This information is stored only on your device. Paycheck has no accounts, doesn't connect to your bank, and never asks for your bank details.

Your device's own iCloud or computer backups may include this data. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple.

What we collect

Nothing. Paycheck contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools. We don't sell or share your data, because we never receive it.

Purchases

Paycheck Pro is sold through Apple. Apple handles your payment and tells the app whether you have Pro. We never see your card details, and Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction.

Notifications

If you turn on bill reminders, danger alerts or the evening check-in, the app schedules those notifications on your phone, along with a birthday message if you added your birthday. They can show your name and amounts from your budget on your lock screen. Nothing is sent through our servers. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone's Settings.

Sharing, photos and exporting

Nothing leaves the app unless you choose to send it. If you share a verdict card or export your spending to a CSV file, it goes wherever you send it, and that app or person's own privacy rules then apply.

If you save a verdict card to your photos, your phone asks permission first. The app only adds that image and doesn't read your photo library.

Deleting your data

Use Plan > Erase my data in the app, or delete the app from your phone. Either one removes your saved numbers from the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing further to request.

Children

Paycheck isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not financial advice

Paycheck does math on the numbers you enter. It isn't financial advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
