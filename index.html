How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Open the Pro screen from the top right of Today, scroll down and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Apple handles subscriptions. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Paycheck. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds too. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where do my numbers go?

Nowhere. They stay on your phone, with no bank login and no account. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I erase my data?

In the app, go to Plan and tap Erase my data. Or delete the app.
